## What the overlay builds

Image tag `tensorfold-glm53:1.1.0-x25`. Shipped series: 86 release + 10 from `patches/` (0909, 0910, 0921, 0923, 0930, 0932, 0933, 0940, 0972, 0980) = **96**; the installer applies it when no 0900 is present. With the optional commdata2338 engine patch (0900) dropped in, the installer also applies the three `patches/needs-0900/` patches (0941, 0970, 0990, in filename order after 0900) and the count becomes 86 + 14 = **100** files — or **99 applied patches** once you swap `0910` for its `0910 x1` variant (the variant replaces the vanilla file, one file fewer). Our production x25 tree is 86 + 0900 + 0910 x1 + the 11 patches from 0921 on = 98 (it does not carry 0909, which only matters for mixed-width packs). Diff of our production scripts against the Aevonix pin: `scripts/diff-vs-aevonix-a190785.txt`.
